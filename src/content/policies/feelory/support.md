Need help with Feelory? Email [apps@caschw.com](mailto:apps@caschw.com).
Feelory is coming soon; there is no announced release date.

## iCloud sync

Use the same Apple Account on your devices, enable sync in Feelory’s Settings,
and restart the app. The first sync may take a few minutes. Your reflections
remain available locally while sync catches up.

Turning sync off does not delete local or previously synced data. See
[Feelory privacy](../privacy/) for deletion and permission choices.

## Backups and exports

Before installing an update or restoring data, export a JSON backup from
Settings. Keep it somewhere private. A PDF is useful for reading, but it is
not a restorable backup.

Deleting content in Feelory does not delete exported files or older device
backups. Manage those separately where you stored them.

## Optional AI

AI features need a supported device with Apple Intelligence available.
You can explore, write, check in, and practice without AI. Suggestions are a
second perspective, not an assessment of what you should feel.

## Report a problem

Include your device, OS version, app version/build, and the steps that led to the
problem. Describe what you expected and what happened. Screenshots can help,
but remove private writing before sharing. Do not send private reflections
or Health data.

The support mailbox is hosted by Outlook.com/Microsoft. We retain correspondence
only while needed to resolve requests, with regular review and deletion of
resolved correspondence. Read [Feelory privacy](../privacy/) for provider
disclosures and how to request deletion of correspondence we hold.

## Urgent help

Feelory is not an emergency service or a substitute for professional care.
If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
In the U.S., call or text **988** for crisis support.
