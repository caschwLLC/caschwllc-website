This policy covers Override, the macOS menu bar app published by caschw LLC.

## Permissions and window management

You choose whether to grant macOS Accessibility access. Override uses supported
macOS interfaces to identify and manage application windows for the features
you enable. You can pause overrides, exclude applications, or revoke access in
System Settings. Website visits do not grant macOS permissions.

## Settings on your Mac

Override stores configuration in macOS preferences, including your enabled
behaviors, shortcuts, layouts, and application rules or exclusions. Removing the
app does not necessarily erase preferences or macOS permission entries. Resetting
settings writes default preferences; it does not guarantee deletion of every
stored preference. You can manage Accessibility access separately in System
Settings.

## Troubleshooting information

Diagnostics are optional and off by default. When enabled, they generate only a
privacy-safe log describing what Override itself was doing, for bug
identification and troubleshooting. No personal information, window titles,
window content, or pointer data is included. When diagnostics are not enabled,
no diagnostics are generated.

Diagnostics stay in memory only while Override is running. Closing (quitting)
the app immediately discards all diagnostics collected in that session.
Diagnostics are copied to the system clipboard only when you choose the report
copy button after enabling diagnostics. Copying a report does not send it to us.
You decide whether to paste it into an email to support.

Any copies you export, save, or share remain wherever you put them. Quitting
Override does not delete those copies.

## No remote collection

Override has no automatic update checks, crash reporting service, remote
services, telemetry, or third-party SDKs. Troubleshooting information reaches
support only if you manually copy and paste it into an email you choose to send.
The website and download host handle their own connection data separately.

## Support and deletion requests

Email [apps@caschw.com](mailto:apps@caschw.com) for support or privacy questions.
Support email is hosted by Outlook.com/Microsoft. We and Microsoft receive your
email address, message, and any details or attachments you choose to send.
Do not send private window or document content.

We keep support correspondence only while needed to resolve requests, with
regular review and deletion of resolved correspondence. To request deletion of
correspondence we hold, email apps@caschw.com. Copies held by Microsoft follow
its own controls and retention policies; we do not promise deletion of
provider-held copies on a specific deadline. See
[Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).
