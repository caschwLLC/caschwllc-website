## Purpose and users

The public caschw LLC software showroom helps prospective app users understand
Feelory and Override, their availability and choices, and find support. It is an
information site, not an account service or storefront.

## Confirmed product truth

Feelory helps with emotional literacy and personal reflection: 128 emotions in
eight families, journaling, multiple feeling tags, quick check-ins, practice and
insights. It is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, with compatible
Macs through Designed for iPad. The public listing
`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/feelory-app/id6819497727` was verified on
2026-10-28. Do not publish its price, rating, review or testimonial content.

Data is local by default. iCloud sync, write-only Apple Health logging and
on-device AI are optional. Core use does not require AI. No remote AI fallback.
Not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment or substitute for professional care.

Override is a macOS menu bar app for configurable window management, available
as **0.1.1 beta** for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 27 or later. Its verified
public DMG is hosted in this website repository's `override-v0.1.1` prerelease.
Window controls, hide/minimize policies, snapping and custom layouts are
optional; Accessibility access is granted by the user in System Settings.
Application support and interception are best-effort. Do not generalize
Feelory's privacy, AI, Health or accessibility claims to Override.
Override's privacy policy uses owner-confirmed app facts: no update checks,
remote services, telemetry, crash reporting service or third-party SDKs.
Optional privacy-safe diagnostics stay in memory until quit and are copied
only by user action. App policies link to the separate website policy.
